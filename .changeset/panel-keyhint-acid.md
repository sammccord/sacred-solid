Add `Panel`, a titled frame with an optional icon, title and meta header and a body. `tone` picks an outline header (a bottom line) or a filled header (`--theme-surface-inverse` with `--theme-ink-inverse` text). Add `KeyHint`, a row of keycaps with a label, drawn in `--theme-control-foreground`. `BarProgress` takes `variant="solid"`, a continuous fill over a track, and keeps `"char"` as the default. Add the `sacred-solid/themes/acid.css` stylesheet with `theme-acid`, a lime on near-black theme with slate highlights and cyan keycaps. It also sets `--font-family-mono` to a Rajdhani stack, so load the font in your page.
